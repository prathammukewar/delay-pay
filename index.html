Late flight? You could be owed €600

Airlines flying in Europe and the UK owe you up to €600 or £520 when you land 3 hours late, lose your flight at short notice or get bumped. Canada pays up to C$1,000 for a delay and C$2,400 if you’re bumped. Check your flight in 30 seconds, then send the claim yourself with a letter that cites the law. Claim companies keep a quarter to half of the payout for sending that same letter.
